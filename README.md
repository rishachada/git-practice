# Source: TechCrunch

What I find most interesting about this article is the debate over whether AI safety should be handled primarily through regulation or by the companies developing these technologies. I found Jensen Huang’s argument that “safety is an engineering problem” particularly interesting because it treats AI like any other technology that can be tested and improved before being released. At the same time, the article points out that even well-intentioned companies can release products with unexpected consequences, which makes me question whether relying entirely on companies and market forces is enough. I also found Salesforce’s development of Koa interesting because it shows how AI is becoming increasingly specialized for specific business tasks rather than simply trying to create one general-purpose model. The fact that Koa uses synthetic data instead of actual customer information also highlights how companies are trying to balance AI capabilities with data privacy and security.
